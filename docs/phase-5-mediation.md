# Phase 5: Guided Self-Mediation & RA Workspace Operations

## Overview
Conflict resolution follows a multi-tier escalation model:
1. **Tier 1 — Guided Self-Mediation**: When pulse check-in detects friction or elevated risk (`medium` or `high`), students are immediately presented with structured self-mediation steps, reference prompts from their Roommate Agreement sections, and pre-composed, non-confrontational message openers.
2. **Tier 2 — RA Mediation Workspace (`/staff/ra`)**:
   - Authorized Resident Advisors (RAs) access a floor-scoped workspace.
   - Visible alerts respect student consent: an alert appears only if the student opted in via `shareWithRa` or explicitly requested help (`askRa`).
   - RAs can record notes, update outcomes (`resolved`, `room_change`, `escalated`), and reopen roommate agreements for mediation.
