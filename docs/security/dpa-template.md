# Data processing agreement (placeholder)

> **Status: placeholder — needs legal review.** Do not send to a school as is.

A school agreement would cover, at least:

- **Parties, purpose and duration.** Roommate matching and housing placement support for the
  school's students; term of the pilot or subscription.
- **Data categories.** As listed in `data-map.md`; school-provided records listed separately.
- **Processor obligations.** Use only for the purpose; confidentiality; staff access by role
  with audit logging; encryption in transit and at rest; subprocessors per `subprocessors.md`
  with notice of changes.
- **Student rights.** Students download their own data (Settings → Your data) and delete their
  account; schools handle records requests in Staff → Privacy & retention.
- **Security incidents.** Notification timelines per `incident-response.md` and applicable law.
- **Return and deletion.** At the end of the agreement, school data is deleted within an agreed
  period; retention settings are configurable per school.
- **Audit.** Reasonable information requests; no third-party certification exists today.
- **Governing law, liability, insurance.** To be drafted by counsel.
