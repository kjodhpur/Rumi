# FERPA "school official" model statement

> **Status: draft — needs legal review.** This is a starting point for a school's counsel, not
> legal advice, and not something a school has agreed to.

Under FERPA (34 CFR § 99.31(a)(1)(i)(B)), a school may disclose education records without
consent to a contractor that performs a service the school would otherwise use employees
for, is under the school's direct control with respect to the records, and uses them only
for the authorized purpose.

**Proposed position.** Rumi is a student-facing service students join voluntarily. Most data
in Rumi is provided by students themselves and is not an education record. Where a school
shares education records with Rumi (for example a StarRez roommate or assignment import), the
school would designate Rumi a school official with a legitimate educational interest under a
written agreement that:

1. Limits use of school-provided records to roommate matching and housing placement support
   for that school.
2. Prohibits re-disclosure except as the school directs or the law requires.
3. Requires deletion or return when the agreement ends (Rumi's retention jobs and staff
   records tools support this; see `data-map.md`).
4. Keeps student-level data from school staff unless the student consents
   (`share_with_housing`), apart from what the school itself provided.

**Open questions for counsel:** whether imported questionnaire answers become education
records once held in Rumi; directory-information opt-outs for imported data; how
"legal basis" exports by staff (Staff → Privacy → Records requests) map to FERPA exceptions.
